## Architecture generalization (context for discussion)

- The head IS the lattice-bosonization phase factor (Chen–Kapustin): a
  discrete gauge structure e^{iπq(fluxes)} attached to flux configurations to
  encode fermion statistics. Hence exact at h=0, expectedly local, robust.
- Wavefunction-side equivalent of the Clifford disentangler (operator-side),
  but cheaper: no dressing of field terms, existing H/sampler untouched.
- Pattern transfers: any decorated/twisted stabilizer target (Walker–Wang-like
  3D models) — stabilizer phases are always GF(2) quadratic forms; h_y bosonic
  runs could take the head as a soft bias; generic frustrated models = same
  architecture pattern with physics-prior phase classes (Marshall-type).
